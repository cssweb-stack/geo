The Department of Geography and Environment benefits from a diverse community of associated faculty whose expertise enriches our teaching, research, and graduate education. Our cooperating, affiliate, adjunct, emeriti, and lecturer faculty bring a wide range of disciplinary perspectives and professional experience, strengthening connections across the university and beyond. Their contributions expand opportunities for interdisciplinary collaboration, mentorship, and the exploration of pressing environmental and societal challenges.
