Geography and Environment at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa

E komo mai — Welcome to the Department of Geography and Environment within the College of Social Sciences at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. Explore how global change shapes communities and environments, and how local actions influence the world around us. The faculty bring expertise in environmental geography, human geography, and geospatial information science, with a focus on Asia and the Pacific. Whether you are discovering geography for the first time or building a research career, find a path that connects your interests with the questions that matter to you.
