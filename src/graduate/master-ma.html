GEO Master of Arts (MA)

Develop Your Geographic Research Skills

The Master of Arts (MA) in Geography and Environment at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa combines geographic study with an original thesis. Core seminars introduce faculty research, geographic concepts, and research design. Work with an advisor to develop a specialization in human geography, environmental geography, or geospatial technologies, and choose methods training suited to your project. Faculty interests across Hawaiʻi, Asia, and the Pacific provide a setting for academic and applied research that builds skills in analysis, writing, and communicating findings.

Master’s at a Glance

  • Degree: Master of Arts (MA), Plan A (thesis)
  • Credits: 30 minimum, including thesis work
  • Program length: Designed for four semesters of full-time study
  • Areas of study: Human geography, environmental geography, and geospatial technologies

Courses and Credits

The MA requires at least 30 credits at the 400 level or higher and combines a foundation in geographic ideas, research methods, a specialization shaped around your interests, and an original thesis. Plan your courses with a faculty advisor as you develop the knowledge and skills to investigate your research question.

{% include "components/divider-medium.njk" %}

Required Core (7 credits)

Meet the faculty, explore the ideas and debates that shape geography, and learn to turn your interests into a research proposal. Three required courses provide the foundation for your graduate work. Earn a B or higher in each:

  • GEO 692: Faculty Seminar Series (1 credit)
  • GEO 695: Concepts and Theory in Geography (3 credits)
  • GEO 696: Research Design / Proposal Writing (3 credits)
{% include "components/divider-medium.njk" %}

Research Skills (3 credits)

Develop methods suited to the questions you want to investigate. Choose approved coursework in quantitative analysis, qualitative research, field methods, or laboratory techniques that supports your specialization and thesis research.

{% include "components/divider-medium.njk" %}

Specialization (12 credits)

Build a focused course plan with your advisor in human geography, environmental geography, or geospatial technologies. Select courses at the 400 level or higher, including at least 6 credits at the 600 or 700 level. Your specialization should connect your interests with faculty expertise. You may include 3 credits from related departments or programs with approval from your advisor and the Graduate Chair.

{% include "components/divider-medium.njk" %}

Thesis (8 credits)

Bring your coursework and research skills together in an original project under your advisor’s guidance. Through GEO 700: Thesis, investigate a geographic problem, gather and analyze evidence, and communicate your findings in a written thesis. Browse MA thesis topics to see the questions students have pursued.

Additional preparation may be required based on your academic background. Review the MA Handbook for breadth requirements, course approvals, and thesis registration guidance.

Plan Your MA

Exploring the Program?

An undergraduate degree in geography is not required. Students from related fields are welcome, although additional coursework may be needed to build a foundation in human and environmental geography, geospatial techniques, or quantitative methods.

Before applying, familiarize yourself with our faculty’s research and identify how your academic interests connect with their expertise. We welcome applicants who can explain that connection and the questions they hope to investigate. Contact the Graduate Chair to discuss potential advisors, program fit, and preparation for graduate study.

{% include "components/divider-white.njk" %}

Ready to Apply?

Review the UH Graduate Division admissions standards and documentation requirements, then prepare your application materials:

  • A 1- to 2-page statement of objectives outlining your research goals, relevant experience, and plans.
  • Three recommendation letters, with academic references preferred.
  • GRE scores (recommended but not required).
  • Academic transcripts as needed from each postsecondary institution attended.
  • English proficiency scores, if required.
  • A writing sample is optional for MA applicants.

Submit your application (fee required) for fall admission only. The application deadline is January 15.

Apply through UH Mānoa Graduate Division

Direct application process questions to UH Graduate Division and program questions to the Graduate Chair.
