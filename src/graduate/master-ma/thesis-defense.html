MA: Thesis & Defense

Review the MA thesis proposal, research, writing, defense, and submission procedures.

Protocols for MA Thesis Defense (October 2020)

Master’s Research Defense

  1. The final defense is an oral examination open to the public, during which the author of a thesis or dissertation demonstrates to their committee satisfactory command of all aspects of the work presented and other related subjects, if applicable.
  2. MA defense should be announced at least two weeks in advance through appropriate listservs.
  3. The student must distribute a copy of the abstract to the faculty of their graduate program. The abstract may be distributed via email or other electronic means as appropriate.
  4. Under COVID-19 special rule, remote participation of the graduate student and all members of the committee, and the general audience is strongly encouraged.
  5. The student candidate, in coordination with the committee chair, is responsible to make prior arrangements for the virtual defense.
  6. Ensure that all materials, including any visual aids, have been distributed in advance to all committee members.

Protocol for the MA Final Defense

  1. The committee chair starts the meeting by welcoming everyone, introducing the candidate, and explaining how the defense will proceed.
  2. The candidate presents the main findings of their thesis research in 35 minutes or less.
  3. After the presentation, the committee chair moderates the discussion following this order:
    1. A brief Q&A to clarify the presentation (questions could be posed by the general audience or the advisory committee).
    2. Q&A on the thesis’s arguments by members of the advisory committee. During this time the audience is welcome to stay and observe the proceeding.
  4. After all questions have been exhausted, the committee meets in a separate room (without the candidate) to discuss:
    1. whether the candidate has satisfactorily produced a defendable manuscript and passed the oral defense (Form 3 will be filed and signed).
    2. what revisions (if any) are required before the candidate can file the final thesis (Form 4 will be signed).
  5. The student is invited to re-join the committee in a closed meeting to hear the committee’s decision and feedback for the final revision.
  6. Beginning Fall 2020, forms are submitted on Kuali online system. This makes the process of obtaining signatures much easier. It also removes any one person to be solely responsible for granting students extension to the filing deadline (listed as the first Friday of April or November each semester; or the second Friday of July for the summer). Please bear this in mind when pushing against the deadline.
