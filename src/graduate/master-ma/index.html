Geography and Environment MA: Overview

The Master of Arts (MA) in Geography and Environment at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa combines geographic study with an original thesis. Core seminars introduce faculty research, geographic concepts, and research design. Work with an advisor to develop a specialization in human geography, environmental geography, or geospatial technologies, and choose methods training suited to your project. Faculty interests across Hawaiʻi, Asia, and the Pacific provide a setting for academic and applied research that builds skills in analysis, writing, and communicating findings.

GEO MA Handbook

The following requirements will apply to all incoming Master’s (MA) students as of the Fall 2017 semester and can be found in your geography MA handbook. Students in residence prior to Fall 2017 will be governed by the requirements in force in the academic year in which they entered the graduate program. While advisory services are available, responsibility for familiarization with, and adherence to, University and Departmental regulations ultimately rests with the doctoral student. For University regulations, consult the Graduate Division as needed. For Departmental regulations, consult the Graduate Chair as needed.

Planning Your Coursework and Research?

Work with your faculty advisor each semester to plan courses, develop your research, and review your progress. Use the MA Handbook to track program milestones, explore graduate funding sources, and check the course resources below when planning your schedule.

Getting Ready to Graduate?

Review your remaining requirements and thesis milestones with your advisor. Check Graduate Division’s graduation instructions and deadlines as you plan your defense, thesis submission, and application for graduation.
