MA: Overview

The Master of Arts (MA) in Geography and Environment at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa combines geographic study with an original thesis. Core seminars introduce faculty research, geographic concepts, and research design. Students work with an advisor to develop a specialization in human geography, environmental geography, or geospatial technologies, and choose methods training suited to your project. Faculty interests across Hawaiʻi, Asia, and the Pacific provide a setting for academic and applied research that builds skills in analysis, writing, and communicating findings.

Program Requirements & Responsibilities

The requirements presented on these MA program pages apply to students entering in fall 2017 or later. Students in residence before fall 2017 follow the requirements in effect during the academic year they entered the graduate program. Although advising is available, MA students are responsible for understanding and following university and departmental regulations. Consult Graduate Division about university regulations and the Graduate Chair about departmental regulations.
