Graduate Applications & Admissions

The Geography and Environment MA and PhD programs welcome applicants whose academic interests align with departmental expertise, including students from related fields. An undergraduate degree in geography is not required, although additional coursework may be needed to build a foundation in human and environmental geography, geospatial techniques, or quantitative methods. Before applying, review faculty research interests and consider how your interests and proposed research questions connect with their work. Contact the Graduate Chair to discuss potential advisors, program fit, and preparation for master’s or doctoral study.

  • The application deadline for Fall admission is January 15 (Fall admissions only).

Application Requirements

Review the UH Graduate Division admissions standards and documentation requirements, then prepare your application materials:

  • A 1- to 2-page statement of objectives outlining your research goals, relevant experience, and plans.
  • Three recommendation letters, with academic references preferred.
  • GRE scores (recommended but not required).
  • Academic transcripts as needed from each postsecondary institution attended.
  • English proficiency scores, if required.
  • Writing sample:
    • A writing sample is optional for MA applicants.
    • A writing sample is required for PhD applicants.

Submit your application (fee required) for fall admission only. The application deadline is January 15.

Apply through UH Mānoa Graduate Division

Direct application process questions to UH Graduate Division and program questions to the Graduate Chair.
