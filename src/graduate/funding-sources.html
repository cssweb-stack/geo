Graduate students may be eligible for funding from the Department of Geography and Environment, the University of Hawaiʻi, and external organizations. Opportunities include assistantships, scholarships, fellowships, research support, and travel awards. The listings below describe funding sources and application procedures; availability and eligibility may vary.

Department Sources

Teaching and Research Assistant Positions

The department awards a limited number of Teaching Assistant positions each year. Applicants for admission will be automatically considered for TAships unless they opt out. For RAships, inquire directly with the faculty sponsors who are Principal Investigators. These positions include tuition remission and a stipend.

Ev Wingert Graduate Field Research Award

Established by the late Professor Ev Wingert, this fund provides limited support (up to $3,000 for PhD and $2,000 for MA) to GEO graduate students for expenses related to thesis or pre-dissertation fieldwork that require travel. Can only be received once.

Deadline: April 1

Full award details and application (UH login required)

Graduate Research and Travel Award

This fund provides small awards, typically $1,000 or less, to support GEO graduate student research expenses or travel to present a paper at a professional conference. Students may receive one Graduate Research and Travel Award per academic year, subject to available funding.

Deadlines: October 1 & February 1

Full award details and application (UH login required)

Doctoral Dissertation Completion Fellowship

This fellowship provides a 9-month graduate assistantship to support one GEO doctoral student in the last year of PhD dissertation writing. Application requirements: Statement describing dissertation research, CV, and endorsement from advisor.

Deadline: March 1

Full award details and application (UH login required)

Abraham Piʻianaiʻa Graduate Scholarship

This fund supports graduate students in the Department of Geography and the Environment engaged in projects related to the geography of Hawaiʻi, reflecting the spirit and life of prominent Hawaiian educator Abraham Piʻianaʻa.

Deadline: February 15

Full award details and application (UH login required)

UH System Sources

Graduate Student Achievement Scholarships (Tuition Support)

The University provides funding to help cover tuition costs for graduate students without tuition remission through an assistantship. Funds vary. Inquire with the Graduate Chair.

Non-Resident Tuition Exemption (Tuition Support)

The University offers a limited number of exemptions for out-of-state residents, reducing their tuition rate to the resident rate.

Area Studies Scholarships

Several area studies centers at UH Mānoa offer a variety of scholarships for students interested in these regions. Check out the Center for Southeast Asian Studies, the Center for South Asian Studies, the Center for Japanese Studies, the Center for Chinese Studies, and the Center for Korean Studies.

Other UH and Affiliated Opportunities

Other funding opportunities are available through the College of Social Sciences, the University, and the East-West Center.

  • East-West Center, Graduate Degree Fellowship
  • College of Social Sciences, Social Science Research Institute (Grant Development)
  • UH Mānoa, Graduate Student Organization (GSO)
  • UH Mānoa, Star Scholarships

External Sources

Many graduate students have received extramural grants and fellowships to support their research. These have included:

  • National Science Foundation, Geography and Spatial Science, Program Doctoral Dissertation Research Improvement Awards
  • Ford Foundation, Fellowship Programs
  • Fulbright US, Student Program Fellowships
  • Social Science Research Council, International Dissertation Research Fellowship (IDRF)
  • US Department of Education, Fulbright-Hays, Doctoral Dissertation Research Abroad
  • US Department of Education, Foreign Language and Area Studies Fellowships Program
