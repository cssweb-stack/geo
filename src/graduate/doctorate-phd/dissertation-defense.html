Geography and Environment PhD: Dissertation & Defense

Review dissertation expectations and the procedures for the final doctoral defense.

Degree Requirements: The Dissertation

The dissertation must demonstrate the student’s ability to formulate a research problem, to assemble and analyze relevant data, to draw appropriate conclusions, and to express findings clearly and concisely. It should be of publishable quality as judged by the Advisory Committee. Students should consult the Graduate Division regarding the document formatting and submission requirements. Students should consult the Graduate Chair regarding document submission to the Department. Consult past dissertations for guides to length and style.

Inclusion of previously published materials is allowed to the extent stipulated by the Graduate Division. The following conditions must be met:

  • The publication must be logically integrated into the manuscript in a coherent manner. Binding reprints of the publication to the manuscript is unacceptable.
  • The manuscript must contain an introduction and conclusion that clearly integrates the publication into the general theme of the manuscript.
  • The abstract for the manuscript must be “comprehensive” in the sense that it covers the manuscript as well as any publication to be included.

In general, the student should be the sole or primary author of any published material to be included. Co-authored material may be included only if the student has made a primary contribution, in terms of both research and writing, and that the nature of the contribution is clearly defined and indicated in the introduction of the dissertation. Written permission shall be obtained from all copyright owners of the co-authored material and attached to the publication agreement of the dissertation as applicable

Protocols for PhD Exams-Proposal-Dissertation Defense (October 2020)

PhD Final Defense

The final defense is an oral examination open to the public, during which the author of a thesis or dissertation demonstrates to his or her committee satisfactory command of all aspects of the work presented and other related subjects, if applicable.

Defense announcement in the University Events Calendar is required for the dissertation. The defense must be one hour or more in length and scheduled between 8:00 am to 4:30 pm on any work day, during both instructional and non-instructional periods. Submit Final Oral Examination for Doctoral Dissertation Defense to the Graduate Division Student Services at least two weeks prior to the defense

The student must distribute a copy of the abstract to the faculty of his or her graduate program. The abstract may be distributed via email or other electronic means as appropriate.

Under a normal f2f situation, a student must submit a Doctoral Petition for Remote Committee Participation if any committee member will participate remotely.

Under COVID-19 special rule, remote participation of the graduate student and all members of the committee is allowed. The graduate chair should submit a memo to graduate student services via email containing the names of the committee members and how the virtual defense will be conducted (no remote committee participation form is needed).

The student candidate, in coordination with the committee chair, is responsible to make prior arrangements for the virtual defense. The link to this defense must be publicly available. All materials, including any visual aids, have been distributed in advance to all committee members.

Protocol for the PhD Final Defense

  • The committee chair starts the meeting by welcoming everyone, introducing the candidate, and explaining how the defense will proceed.
  • The candidate presents the main findings of her/his dissertation research in 45 minutes or less.
  • After the presentation, the committee chair moderates the discussion following this order:
  • A brief Q&A to clarify the presentation (questions could be posed by the general audience or the advisory committee)
  • Q&A on the dissertation’s arguments by members of the advisory committee. At this time the general audience is welcome to stay and observe the proceeding.
  • After all questions have been exhausted, the committee meets in a separate room (without the candidate) to discuss:
  • whether the candidate has satisfactorily produced a defendable manuscript and passed the oral defense (Form 3 will be initiated and signed).
  • what revisions (if any) are required before the candidate can file the final dissertation (Form 4 will be initiated and signed).
  • The student is invited to re-join the committee, without the public, to hear the committee’s decision and feedback for the final revision.
  • Beginning Fall 2020, forms are submitted on Kuali online system. This makes the process of obtaining signatures much easier. It also removes any one person to be solely responsible for granting students extension to the filing deadline (listed as the first Friday of April or November each semester; or the second Friday of July for the summer). Please bear this in mind when pushing against the deadline.
