PhD: Overview

The Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Geography and Environment at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa centers on independent research and an original contribution to geographic knowledge. Students develop an individually arranged specialization with a faculty advisor and advisory committee, deepening their command of its literature, theories, and methods. Research training can include GIS, remote sensing, qualitative inquiry, or fieldwork. Coursework, comprehensive exams, and proposal development prepare students to undertake a dissertation and communicate their findings through writing and a public defense.

Academic Objectives

The Geography and Environment doctoral (PhD) degree provides training for those seeking to advance their understanding of concepts and methodologies to the standards of professional geographers. Doctoral students are expected to develop the following during their course of study:

  • Basic understanding of the discipline of geography, its relationship with cognate fields, and its contribution to knowledge. This entails gaining a working knowledge of general geographic literature, familiarity with the structure of the discipline and sub-disciplines, and principal philosophical approaches and concepts.
  • Detailed understanding of at least one specialty within the discipline of geography. This entails gaining a thorough knowledge of the specialty’s historical development, associated literature, main theories, and empirical findings.
  • Ability to conduct independent research of a professional quality. This entails gaining theoretical and practical knowledge of specific research techniques and demonstrating this knowledge in the conduct of original research.
  • Ability to communicate the results of research in both oral and written forms. This entails demonstrating skills in oral presentation and the writing of formal papers during coursework, and ultimately, a dissertation.
  • Familiarity with and adherence to the codes of practice established for academic study at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa including Campus Policies, Academic Policies, and the Student Code of Conduct.
