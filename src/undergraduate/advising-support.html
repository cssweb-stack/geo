Advising & Support

Get help choosing courses, planning your studies, and understanding program and general education requirements. Advising is available for the GIS certificate, the Geography and Environment minor and major, and the GEO BA/MA and Sustainability BA/GEO MA combined degree pathways (BAM).

Plan and Complete Your GIS Certificate

To pursue the GIS certificate, complete the GIS Certificate Application and schedule an appointment with the Undergraduate Chair to review completed courses and plan future coursework.

During the semester you finish your certificate coursework, meet with the Undergraduate Chair to approve your completed program and obtain the required signatures. Submit the signed form with your graduation paperwork to your major college advising office.

Declare a Minor or Major

Before submitting a declaration, talk with the Undergraduate Chair about your interests, completed courses, and how the GEO minor or major (BA) fits your academic plans. Consult the General Education Advisor about university and college requirements and overall degree planning.

Use the major and minor declaration form to request the GEO minor or major (UH login required). Follow the form’s instructions and complete any required approvals. The Registrar’s declaration guidance explains the process and effective dates. If you are adding GEO as an additional major, contact the appropriate college advising office for instructions.

BAM Pathways

The GEO BA/MA pathway and Sustainability BA/GEO MA pathway combine undergraduate and graduate study. Contact the Undergraduate Chair for undergraduate GEO course and program questions, and the Graduate Chair for guidance on the graduate portion of either pathway.
