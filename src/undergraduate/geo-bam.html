GEO BA/MA (BAM)

Take Your Geographic Research Further

The combined BA/MA in Geography and Environment allows you to deepen your study of places, people, and the environments that connect them. Explore how cultural, political, and economic forces interact with natural systems, how geography shapes human experiences, and how these relationships change over time. Build on your undergraduate foundation in human geography, environmental geography, or geospatial information science as you develop advanced research skills, investigate questions that matter to you, and produce original scholarship through a master's thesis.

BAM at a Glance

  • Degrees: Bachelor of Arts (BA) and Master of Arts (MA) in Geography and Environment
  • Program length: Approximately five years, with the BA typically completed after four years and the MA after an additional year
  • Total credits: A minimum of 141 distinct credits, satisfying the requirements of a 120-credit BA and a 30-credit MA through 9 approved shared credits
  • Master's degree: Plan A (thesis), emphasizing advanced research and original scholarship
  • Gateway course: GEO 325: Geography, Environment, and Society, completed with a grade of B or better before applying
  • Minimum GPA: 3.0 cumulative GPA required for admission to and continuation in the graduate program
  • Application timing: Typically during the junior year, with a March 15 deadline for fall admission

ⓘAdditional university and program requirements apply. Review the official UH Mānoa BAM guidelines and consult your BAM Coordinator to confirm current eligibility, deadlines, and degree requirements.

BAM Advantages

One of the key benefits of the BAM pathway is the ability to count up to three approved courses (9 credits) toward both your bachelor's and master's degrees. This shared coursework offers two important advantages:

  • Save time: Complete both degrees in approximately five years instead of the traditional six, potentially earning your master's degree just one to one-and-a-half years after completing your bachelor's degree.
  • Save money: Take approved graduate coursework at undergraduate tuition rates while completing your bachelor's degree, potentially reducing the total cost of earning both degrees.

Courses and Credits

The GEO BAM brings together the requirements of the undergraduate BA and the research-focused MA. Students first meet the gateway requirement, then complete specific approved courses that may count toward both degrees. The credit totals and course choices below show how the pathway fits together.

Degree Credit Requirements

The pathway combines a 120-credit BA and a 30-credit MA. With all 9 approved shared credits applied to both degrees, students earn a minimum of 141 distinct credits.

  • BA: 120 credits, including at least 31 GEO credits and the university’s applicable upper-division requirements.
  • MA: 30 credits in the Plan A (thesis) program, including at least 21 credits not double-counted.
  • Shared coursework: Up to 9 approved credits may satisfy requirements for both degrees.
{% include "icons/book.svg" %}

Gateway and Shared Courses

Complete the gateway course before applying. Students admitted to the pathway may then take the approved shared courses during undergraduate study. A grade of B or better is required in each course used for double counting. Plan your shared courses with the GEO BAM Coordinator before enrolling to confirm university approval and degree applicability.

Gateway course

  • GEO 325: Geography, Environment, and Society (3 credits) — B or better required for consideration; passing does not guarantee admission.

Approved shared coursework (up to 9 credits)

  • GEO 695: Concepts and Theories in Geography (3 credits)
  • GEO 696: Research Design/Methods in Geography (3 credits)
  • One approved 400-level GEO specialization course (3 credits), selected from the options below

Approved Specialization Courses

Choose one approved 400-level course that connects to your interests in human geography, environmental geography, or geospatial information science. Only the courses listed below are identified for this shared-course requirement.

Human Geography

  • GEO 425: Geographies of Popular Culture
  • GEO 426: Environment, Resources and Society

Environmental Geography

  • GEO 401: Climate Change
  • GEO 411: Past Global Change and the Human Era

Geospatial Information Science

  • GEO 470: Remote Sensing
  • GEO 489: GIS for Environmental Sciences

Program Requirements and Policies

Along with completing the GEO BA and Plan A MA requirements, BAM students must meet university rules governing admission, shared coursework, and graduate enrollment. Keep these important conditions in mind as you plan:

  • Academic standing: Maintain a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0 for admission to and continuation in the graduate program. Meeting minimum eligibility requirements does not guarantee admission.
  • Course grades and approval: Earn a B or better in GEO 325 before applying and in each course used for double counting. Shared courses must be approved for the GEO pathway, taken at UH Mānoa, and cannot be replaced with unapproved substitutes.
  • Undergraduate degree completion: Meet all BA requirements, including at least 45 upper-division credits and the required GEO major coursework. The BA must be conferred before you can continue as a classified graduate student.
  • Graduate status and enrollment: After the BA is awarded, graduate tuition and Graduate Division enrollment policies apply. Continuous enrollment is normally required; leave-of-absence procedures depend on whether you have completed the BA.

ⓘThis is a summary, not a complete statement of university policy. Review the official UH Mānoa combined pathway guidelines and BAM student FAQs, and confirm current requirements with your BAM Coordinator.

Applications and Admissions

Interested in GEO BAM? Start planning early so the gateway course, graduate application, and approved shared coursework fit your degree schedule.

Explore the Pathway

Ideally during your sophomore year, meet with the GEO BAM Coordinator to discuss your interests, academic progress, and course plan. Transfer students should seek guidance as early as possible.

Prepare to Apply

Complete GEO 325 with a B or better before applying. Students typically seek admission during their junior year. The application deadline for fall admission is March 15. Confirm the current deadline, eligibility requirements, and application materials with the BAM Coordinator before applying.

Submit Your Application

With the coordinator’s guidance, submit the UH Mānoa Graduate Division application, application fee, and any department-required materials by the deadline. If admitted, follow university instructions for accepting admission and planning your shared courses.
