Take your interest in geography further. The combined BA/MA pathway in Geography and Environment connects undergraduate study with graduate research, giving you a way to build toward both degrees through a coordinated academic plan. Designed as a five-year pathway, GEO BAM lets eligible students begin approved coursework that counts toward both degrees while completing their BA. It is an opportunity to deepen your understanding of people, places, and environmental change and develop a stronger foundation for research and professional work.

Build Toward Graduate Research

Move from exploring geographic questions to developing a focused research interest. Connect your undergraduate studies in human geography, environmental geography, or geospatial information science with graduate work in geographic concepts and research methods.

The GEO pathway combines the BA and MA in Geography and Environment and leads to the Plan A master’s degree. Talk with the department about your interests and how they could develop into a graduate research project.

The GEO Pathway

Gateway Course

Complete GEO 325: Geography, Environment, and Society (3 credits) with a grade of B or better to be considered for the pathway. Completing the gateway course does not by itself guarantee admission.

Approved Shared Courses

GEO identifies the following coursework for up to 9 credits of double counting toward the BA and MA, subject to university BAM requirements:

  • GEO 695: Concepts and Theories in Geography (3 credits)
  • GEO 696: Research Design/Methods in Geography (3 credits)
  • One approved 400-level course (3 credits) aligned with your specialization, selected from the choices below

Human Geography

  • GEO 425: Geographies of Popular Culture
  • GEO 426: Environment, Resources and Society

Environmental Geography

  • GEO 401: Climate Change
  • GEO 411: Past Global Change and the Human Era

Geospatial Information Science

  • GEO 470: Remote Sensing
  • GEO 489: GIS for Environmental Sciences

Plan your shared coursework with the department before enrolling.

GEO BAM Sample Plan

Plan Your Pathway

  1. Start with the department. Contact the Undergraduate and Graduate Chairs to discuss your research interests, the gateway course, and whether GEO BAM fits your academic plans.
  2. Review university BAM requirements. Use the university resource below for eligibility, GPA and grade requirements, tuition, enrollment policies, and the general application process.
  3. Prepare your GEO application. Confirm the current Geography application deadline and required program materials with the department before submitting your Graduate Division application.
