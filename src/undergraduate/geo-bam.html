GEO BA/MA (BAM)

The Department of Geography and Environment offers a combined Bachelor of Arts (BA) and Master of Arts (MA), Plan A, pathway that allows students to earn both degrees in five years.

BAM Advantages

One of the key benefits of the BAM pathway is the ability to count up to three approved courses (9 credits) toward both your bachelor's and master's degrees. This shared coursework offers two important advantages:

  • Save time: Earn both degrees in five years through the combined pathway.
  • Undergraduate tuition: Begin double-counted coursework at undergraduate tuition rates in the fall of senior year.

General Requirements

The pathway includes requirements for academic standing, completion of the bachelor’s degree, graduate enrollment, and leaves of absence.

  • Academic standing: An overall cumulative GPA of at least 3.0 upon graduation with the bachelor’s degree is required to apply for and continue in the graduate program.
  • Bachelor’s degree completion: Complete all required coursework, including at least 45 upper-division credits and at least 31 GEO credits, to receive the BA.
  • Graduate enrollment: Students begin their ninth semester as graduate students and continue the master’s program at graduate tuition rates. Continuous enrollment after the BA is conferred is required unless students are on an approved leave of absence after their first semester as graduate students. If the BA is not conferred, graduate status will be inactivated, and students may not continue taking courses applicable to the graduate program.
  • Leaves of absence: Before the BA is conferred, follow undergraduate procedures to request a leave of absence. Once classified as graduate students, students must be enrolled for at least one semester before becoming eligible for a leave of absence.

Courses and Credits

The pathway combines BA and Plan A MA requirements, with a gateway course and specific courses that count toward both degrees.

Degree Credit Requirements

The pathway combines a 120-credit BA and a 30-credit MA. With all 9 approved shared credits applied to both degrees, students earn a minimum of 141 distinct credits.

  • BA: 120 credits, including at least 31 GEO credits and the university’s applicable upper-division requirements.
  • MA: 30 credits in the Plan A program.
  • Shared coursework: Up to 9 approved credits may satisfy requirements for both degrees.
{% include "media/icons/book.svg" %}

Gateway and Shared Courses

Undergraduate majors must complete GEO 325 with a grade of B or better to be considered for the pathway. Up to nine credits may count toward both degrees: two 600-level courses and one 400-level course, passed with a grade of B or better.

Gateway course

  • GEO 325: Geography, Environment, and Society (3 credits), with a grade of B or better required for consideration.

Approved shared coursework (up to 9 credits)

  • GEO 695: Concepts and Theories in Geography (3 credits)
  • GEO 696: Research Designs / Methods in Geography (3 credits)
  • One approved 400-level GEO specialization course (3 credits), selected from the options below
{% include "media/icons/layers.svg" %}

Approved Specialization Courses

Select one course from the following options that fits your specialization. Each course is worth 3 credits.

Human Geography

  • GEO 425: Geographies of Popular Culture
  • GEO 426: Environment, Resources and Society

Environmental Geography

  • GEO 401: Climate Change
  • GEO 411: Past Global Change and the Human Era

Geospatial Information Science

  • GEO 470: Remote Sensing
  • GEO 489: GIS for Environmental Sciences

Applications and Admissions

Submit the Graduate Admissions Application, application fee, and all required program admission materials by March 15 for fall admission in the same year.

Accepted students typically receive admission notification in the spring of their junior year (sixth semester). They begin approved shared coursework the following fall (seventh semester) at undergraduate tuition rates and are considered dual program students.
