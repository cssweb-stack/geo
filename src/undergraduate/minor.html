Minor in Geography and Environment

The minor in Geography and Environment at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa adds a geographic perspective to your major through coursework in human geography, environmental geography, and geospatial technologies. Choose from subjects such as tourism, food systems, climate, water, GIS, and remote sensing. Study how societies use resources, how physical environments change, or how maps and spatial data reveal geographic patterns. The minor provides breadth across these areas while leaving room for courses that complement your main field of study.

Requirements

The minor in Geography and Environment requires 15 credits of upper-division geography coursework, including at least one course in each of three areas: human geography, environmental geography, and geospatial technologies. Select additional upper-division geography courses to complete the 15-credit total.

Study how culture, politics, economies, and everyday life shape places and communities. Explore connections among tourism, food systems, cities, and the environment.

  • GEO 432: Tea and Culture

  • GEO 403: Fluvial Geomorphology

  • GEO 409: Cultural Biogeography

  • GEO 472: Field Mapping

  • GEO 487: GIS and Spatial Analysis for Social Sciences
  • GEO 489: GIS for Environmental Sciences
