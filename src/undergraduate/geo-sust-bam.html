SUST BA/GEO MA (BAM)

In collaboration with the Institute for Sustainability and Resilience, the Interdisciplinary Studies Program and Department of Geography and Environment offer a combined BA in Interdisciplinary Studies: Sustainability and MA in Geography and Environment (Plan A) pathway that allows students to earn both degrees in five years.

BAM Advantages

One of the key benefits of the BAM pathway is the ability to count up to three approved courses (9 credits) toward both your bachelor's and master's degrees. This shared coursework offers two important advantages:

  • Save time: Earn both degrees in five years through the combined pathway.
  • Undergraduate tuition: Begin double-counted coursework at undergraduate tuition rates in the fall of senior year.

General Requirements

Students must complete the gateway course and meet the pathway’s academic standing requirement.

  • Gateway course: Complete GEO 325 / SUST 326: Geography, Environment and Society (3 credits) with a grade of B or better to be eligible. This course is part of the Upper-Division Major Core Courses for the Interdisciplinary Studies: Sustainability degree.
  • Academic standing: An overall cumulative GPA of at least 3.0 upon graduation with the bachelor’s degree is required to apply for and continue in the graduate program.

Courses and Credits

The pathway combines the BA in Interdisciplinary Studies: Sustainability and the MA in Geography and Environment, with nine credits that may count toward both degrees.

Degree Credit Requirements

The pathway combines a 120-credit BA and a 30-credit MA. With all 9 approved shared credits applied to both degrees, students earn a minimum of 141 distinct credits.

  • BA: 120 credits in Interdisciplinary Studies: Sustainability.
  • MA: 30 credits in the Plan A program.
  • Shared coursework: Up to 9 approved credits may satisfy requirements for both degrees.
{% include "media/icons/book.svg" %}

Gateway and Shared Courses

GEO 325 / SUST 326: Geography, Environment and Society (3 credits) is the gateway course, with a grade of B or better required for eligibility. The following coursework may count toward both degrees:

  • GEO 695: Concepts and Theories in Geography (3 credits), counted as an Upper-Division Major Core Course in the Interdisciplinary Studies: Sustainability major.
  • GEO 696: Research Design / Methods in Geography (3 credits), substituted for SUST 495: Sustainability Capstone (3 credits).
  • One 400-level course that fits both the sustainability major area of concentration and the Geography and Environment MA, selected from the options below.
{% include "media/icons/layers.svg" %}

Approved Specialization Courses

Select one course that fits both programs. Each course is worth 3 credits.

  • GEO 411 / SUST 413: Past Global Change and the Human Era
  • GEO 426 / SUST 426: Environment, Resources, and Society
  • GEO 422 / SUST 423: Agriculture, Food and Society
  • SUST 427 / ERTH 420: Beaches, Reefs, and Climate Change
  • SUST 420 / NREM 420: Community and Natural Resource Management

Applications and Admissions

Submit the Graduate Admissions Application, application fee, and all required program admission materials by March 15 for fall admission in the same year.

Accepted students typically receive admission notification in the spring of their junior year (sixth semester). They begin approved shared coursework the following fall (seventh semester) at undergraduate tuition rates and are considered dual program students.

For more information, contact uhmgeo@hawaii.edu or sustain@hawaii.edu.
