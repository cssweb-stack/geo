Turn your interest in sustainability into a deeper understanding of how people and environments shape one another. The SUST BA/GEO MA (BAM) pathway connects interdisciplinary undergraduate study with graduate research in Geography and Environment. Explore questions of climate change, food systems, resource management, and community well-being while building the research skills to investigate them. Designed as a five-year pathway, it gives eligible students a coordinated route toward a sustainability bachelor’s degree and a geography master’s degree, with approved coursework counting toward both.

Connect Sustainability With Geographic Research

Bring an interdisciplinary perspective to a focused research question. Geographic approaches help you examine how environmental change, social relationships, and decisions about resources connect across places and scales.

This pathway combines the BA in Interdisciplinary Studies: Sustainability with the MA in Geography and Environment (Plan A). It is offered through collaboration among the Department of Geography and Environment, the Interdisciplinary Studies Program, and the Institute for Sustainability and Resilience.

The Combined Pathway

Gateway Course

Complete GEO 325 / SUST 326: Geography, Environment, and Society (3 credits) with a grade of B or better to be considered for the pathway. Completing the gateway course does not by itself guarantee admission.

Approved Shared Courses

The pathway identifies the following coursework for up to 9 credits of double counting toward the BA and MA, subject to university BAM requirements:

  • GEO 695: Concepts and Theories in Geography (3 credits) counts toward the sustainability major’s upper-division core.
  • GEO 696: Research Design/Methods in Geography (3 credits) substitutes for SUST 495: Sustainability Capstone within this pathway.
  • One approved 400-level course (3 credits) fits both your sustainability concentration and the geography master’s degree, selected from the following choices.
  • GEO 411 / SUST 413: Past Global Change and the Human Era
  • GEO 426 / SUST 426: Environment, Resources, and Society
  • GEO 422 / SUST 423: Agriculture, Food and Society
  • SUST 427 / ERTH 420: Beaches, Reefs, and Climate Change
  • SUST 420 / NREM 420: Community and Natural Resource Management

Plan your shared coursework with the Geography Graduate Chair and your sustainability advisor before enrolling.

Plan Your Pathway

  1. Discuss your interests. Contact the Geography Graduate Chair and sustainability advising to explore how the pathway fits your undergraduate concentration and research interests.
  2. Review university BAM requirements. Use the university resource below for eligibility, GPA and grade requirements, tuition, enrollment policies, and the general application process.
  3. Prepare your application. Confirm the current program deadline and required materials with your advisors before submitting your Graduate Division application.
