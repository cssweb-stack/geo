A Message from the Chair

When you give to GEO, you support the work our students are doing now and the researchers they are becoming. Your generosity helps undergraduate and graduate students pursue research, present their findings at conferences, and exchange ideas through events such as our Annual Geography Symposium. These experiences are an important part of their education. Thank you for helping us create opportunities for our students and for being part of the GEO community.
