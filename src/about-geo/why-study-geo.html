Why Study GEO

Explore Your World. Discover Your Direction

Why do places change? How do people and environments shape each other? And how can we build a more sustainable, equitable future? Geography connects these questions to the places and people around us. At UH Mānoa, explore the world through environmental geography, human geography, and geospatial information science, with Hawaiʻi and the Pacific offering a distinctive place to begin.

Reasons to Study with Us

Find a place to explore your questions, put ideas into practice, and build a direction of your own, whether you are beginning an undergraduate program or looking ahead to graduate research.

Study in an Extraordinary Place

In Hawaiʻi, the connections among land, water, communities, and culture are close at hand. Explore how coastlines, food systems, tourism, urban growth, and climate change connect everyday life here to wider changes across Asia and the Pacific. Study geography in a place that invites you to look closely, consider different perspectives, and understand how local experiences connect to global processes, always with attention to the people who call these places home.

Take Your Learning into the World

A map can reveal a pattern. A field observation can challenge an assumption. Geography brings observation, data, and interpretation together to investigate questions about ecosystems, climate, communities, and land use. Through course projects, mapping, and research, learn to turn your curiosity into an investigation and communicate what you discover. Explore current fieldwork, research, and practicum opportunities to connect your studies with the world beyond the classroom.

Build Skills That Open Doors

Build the ability to see patterns, evaluate evidence, and communicate a clear argument, alongside practical skills shaped by your course choices in GIS, cartography, remote sensing, spatial analysis, and field research. Apply those tools to social and environmental questions while developing work that demonstrates what you can contribute. These skills can support paths into planning, conservation, public service, community organizations, geospatial work, or further study.

Find Your Community

Bring your curiosity, your experiences, and the questions you care about. Geography connects people with interests ranging from the processes shaping landscapes to the choices shaping communities, creating opportunities to exchange ideas and learn from different perspectives. Get to know the department through courses, public lectures, and conversations with students and faculty, and seek guidance as you find your direction in an undergraduate program or explore a graduate research path.

A Message from the Chair

We are proud of the curiosity and commitment our students bring to geography. Our goal is to help you connect your interests with meaningful questions, develop skills you can carry forward, and find your place in our academic community. We look forward to learning what matters to you and helping you explore where geography can take you.

Qi Chen
Professor and Department Chair
