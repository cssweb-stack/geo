Understand Environments. Explore Societies. Map Connections

Explore the connections among people, places, and the environment. At UH Mānoa, students bring together environmental geography, human geography, and geospatial information science to understand a changing world, from climate and ecosystems to communities, resources, and land use, with particular attention to Hawaiʻi and the Asia-Pacific region.
