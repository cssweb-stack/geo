Why Study Geography and Environment?

Why do places change? How do people and environments shape each other? And how can we build a more sustainable, equitable future? Geography connects these questions to the places and people around us. At UH Mānoa, explore the world through environmental geography, human geography, and geospatial information science, with Hawaiʻi and the Pacific offering a distinctive place to begin.

Undergraduate Studies

GEO students study the crucial interactions between humankind and the biophysical environment. They obtain a holistic understanding of our world and the fieldwork/research tools needed for a wide range of local, national, and international career opportunities, including environmental planning, natural resource management, and social policy development.

Our geography undergraduate program offers three interrelated themes of study:

  1. Environmental Geography – A systematic study of the Earth’s physical nature and the human activities that change it.
  2. Human Geography – A focus on the cultural, economic, environmental, and political processes that shape human experience and sense of place.
  3. Geospatial Information Science – The use of GIS, remote sensing, and cartography to investigate environmental and societal issues.

Graduate Studies

Students who enter the geography graduate program at UH Mānoa participate in world-class academic and applied geographic research under our energetic faculty's guidance. Geography graduates are well situated to begin exciting careers in public and private sectors and higher education environments.
