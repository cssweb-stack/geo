This policy explains how the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa Department of Geography and Environment website uses information about visits to the site. It applies to this department website; other university websites and external services may have their own privacy policies.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people find and use our website and to improve its content and navigation. Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, links selected, files downloaded, referring websites, browser and device details, and approximate geographic location.

Google Analytics uses cookies, including identifiers that help distinguish visits. Google processes analytics information on our behalf. Google states that Analytics does not log or store individual IP addresses; IP addresses may be used during collection to determine approximate location. This does not mean that a visit is anonymous or that other services, such as the website host, do not process IP addresses.

Our purpose in using analytics is to evaluate and improve this website. For more about Google's processing, see how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your privacy choices

  • Opt out of Google Analytics: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on in a supported browser. It prevents supported Google Analytics scripts from sharing visit activity with Google Analytics in that browser.
  • Manage cookies: Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking cookies can limit cookie-based measurement, but does not necessarily prevent all analytics requests.
  • Use browser privacy controls: Your browser may offer tracking protection or extensions that block analytics requests. Availability and behavior vary by browser.

The Google Analytics add-on applies to the browser where it is installed. It does not stop information from reaching the website itself, prevent hosting or security logs, or control other services. You may need to adjust settings separately on each browser and device you use.

Google Search Console

We use Google Search Console to understand how this website appears in Google Search, including search impressions, clicks, and search terms reported by Google. These reports help us improve how people find our content. Search Console is separate from the Google Analytics tracking used on this website; the Analytics opt-out add-on does not control Google's collection of activity on Google Search.

Information you send us

If you email the department, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that information to respond to your inquiry and may direct it to university staff who can help. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information through ordinary email.

Other services and websites

Our website links to university resources and external websites. It also loads some website assets, such as stylesheets and scripts, from external providers. Your browser sends technical information, including your IP address, when requesting those resources. Those providers' privacy policies govern their processing.

For broader university information, read the UH Mānoa Privacy Statement.

Questions and updates

For questions about this policy, email cssweb@hawaii.edu. We may update this page when website services or practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
